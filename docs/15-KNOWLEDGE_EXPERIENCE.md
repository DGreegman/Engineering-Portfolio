# 15 — Knowledge Experience

## Purpose

The Knowledge section is the heart of the Engineering Workspace.

Its purpose is not to function as a blog.

Its purpose is to become a long-term engineering reference that helps readers understand software engineering concepts, architectural decisions, and implementation trade-offs.

Every design decision should optimize for learning rather than publishing.

---

# Learning Philosophy

Articles should answer questions.

They should explain trade-offs.

They should encourage deeper understanding rather than quick consumption.

Readers should leave with better engineering judgment, not simply more information.

The experience should feel closer to reading an engineering handbook than browsing a news feed.

---

# Knowledge Architecture

The Knowledge section consists of four layers.

Engineering Knowledge
        ↓
Topic
        ↓
Article
        ↓
Related Learning

This hierarchy allows readers to naturally move between connected ideas.

---

# Reading Experience

Long-form reading should always feel comfortable.

Guidelines:

Readable line length

Generous whitespace

Comfortable typography

Clear heading hierarchy

Fast page loads

No visual distractions

Content should always remain the primary focus.

---

# Table of Contents

Every long article should automatically generate a table of contents.

Requirements:

Sticky on desktop

Collapsible on mobile

Highlights current section

Supports nested headings

---

# Code Blocks

Syntax highlighting

# Diagrams

# Images

Avoid decorative imagery.

# Series

Some topics naturally span multiple articles.

Example:

Authentication Fundamentals

Part 1

Part 2

Part 3

Series navigation should clearly communicate progress.

---

# Backlinks

Whenever articles reference one another, surface those relationships.

Readers should easily discover connected knowledge.

---

# Reading Progress

Long-form articles should include a reading progress indicator.

The indicator should remain subtle and never distract from the content.

---

# Empty States

Every empty state should encourage exploration rather than simply stating that content is unavailable.

---

# Accessibility

The Knowledge section should remain fully accessible.

Requirements:

Semantic headings

Keyboard navigation

Screen reader compatibility

High contrast

Accessible code blocks

Accessible diagrams

---

# Mobile Experience

The reading experience should remain excellent on mobile devices.

The table of contents should collapse into an accessible drawer.

Typography should remain optimized for long-form reading.

---

# Future Vision

The long-term goal is to create an engineering library rather than a collection of articles.

Readers should naturally move from one topic to another, building a deeper understanding over time.

The experience should encourage curiosity, exploration, and continuous learning.

Success is measured not by the number of articles published, but by the clarity, usefulness, and longevity of the knowledge shared.
