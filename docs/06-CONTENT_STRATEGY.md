Version: 1.0
Status: Draft

# Purpose

This document defines how content is planned, created, reviewed, published, updated, and maintained.

The objective is not simply to publish articles.

The objective is to continuously build credibility as an engineer.

Every piece of content should answer at least one of these questions:

• What have I built?

• What have I learned?

• What can I teach?

• How do I think as an engineer?

---

# Content Mission

The portfolio should become the single source of truth for all engineering knowledge created by Gracious Obeagu.

Social media platforms distribute ideas.

The portfolio owns the knowledge.

# AI Collaboration Policy

AI may assist with:

Grammar

Research

Structure

Editing

Brainstorming

Example generation

AI must never fabricate:

Metrics

Challenges

Lessons learned

Production incidents

Every published article should accurately reflect the author's knowledge and experience.

---

# Content Review Checklist

Before publishing, ask:

Is it technically accurate?

Is it useful?

Would I recommend this to another engineer?

Does it include practical examples?

Does it connect to other knowledge?

Could this still be valuable in one year?

---

# Publishing Frequency

Quality takes priority over quantity.

Target cadence:

1 Engineering Article per week

1 Engineering Log every 1–2 weeks

1 Case Study whenever a major project is completed

Security Notes as learning progresses

---

# Measuring Success

The goal is not page views.

Success is measured by:

Increasing technical depth.

Building trust.

Helping other engineers.

Supporting career growth.

Creating a long-term engineering knowledge base.

---

100+ engineering articles
