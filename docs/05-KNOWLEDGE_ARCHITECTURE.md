# Purpose

This document defines how engineering knowledge is created, organized, connected, published, maintained, and discovered throughout the portfolio.

The portfolio is not a blog.

It is an Engineering Knowledge Base.

Every piece of content should contribute to a growing body of engineering knowledge that demonstrates expertise, curiosity, and continuous learning.

---

# Knowledge Philosophy

Knowledge compounds.

The value of this portfolio should increase over time.

Each new article, project, or experiment should strengthen the entire ecosystem rather than exist in isolation.

Visitors should feel like they are exploring an engineering library.

# Editorial Standards

Every article should answer:

Why does this matter?

What problem does it solve?

How does it work?

What mistakes should people avoid?

How is this used in production?

What did I personally learn?

---

# Writing Principles

Teach.

Don't impress.

Explain.

Don't overwhelm.

Show examples.

Prefer diagrams over long explanations.

Avoid unnecessary jargon.
